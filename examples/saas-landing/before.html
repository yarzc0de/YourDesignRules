How it works

Fewer 3 a.m. pages. Cleaner handoffs.

When your team spans continents, someone is always starting their day. Sunshift makes sure that's who gets the alert.

A handoff note at every shift change

When one shift ends, Sunshift posts a note in Slack for the next person, so nobody starts their shift by scrolling through yesterday's channel.

  • Open incidents and where they stand
  • Recent deploys from GitHub
  • Alerts that have been noisy

Unanswered pages don't sit there

If a page isn't acknowledged within 5 minutes, Sunshift sends it to the next person. Five minutes is the default; set whatever window fits your team.
