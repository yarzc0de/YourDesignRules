Shift sleep plan

Import your rota. Get a plan built around it.

Generic sleep advice assumes you go to bed at night. Tidewell starts from your actual shift calendar and works out the rest.

When to sleep

Your main sleep, placed around the shift you just finished and the one coming next.

When to nap

A nap slotted in before a first night or a long stretch, so you start the shift rested.

When to avoid light

The windows to stay out of bright light, like the drive home after a night shift.
